# On a phone

On a small screen the background fills the screen and the controls sit in one bar at the bottom:

- **Layers** opens the layer list as a sheet. Tap it again, or tap the background, to close it.
- **Edit** opens the inspector for the selected layer, with the same **Layer** and **Document** tabs as on a computer.
- **Play** plays and pauses.
- **Presets** opens the preset gallery.

Parameter fields work by dragging sideways, so the sheet still scrolls when you swipe up and down. Tap a field to type a value.

Saving downloads the file, and the share link button in the top bar is the easiest way to move a background to your computer.
